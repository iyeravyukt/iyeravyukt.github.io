AUV torpedo launcher

Mechanical design · Underwater systems

Jun–Aug 2025MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute

My roleLauncher design & testing

A pin-actuated compressed-air launcher reached 86% launch success in submerged trials.

Launcher on the AUV
Launcher CAD
Demo GIF or video

Problem

The launcher had to fire consistently at varying depths while staying waterproof and protecting the vehicle's electronics.

My contribution

Designed and built the launcher with a pressure-regulated air reservoir, magnetic pin, and reinforced barrel. A magnetic switch drives a needle, nested in a 3D-printed housing, that punctures the air canister.

Result

86% launch success in submerged trials and reliable performance at up to 15 ft of depth.

Details

  • The mounting interface was modeled in CAD to minimize drag.
  • Torpedo geometry was refined through on-site testing to reduce backflow turbulence and keep propulsion consistent across depths.

  • 3D printing
  • Waterproofing
