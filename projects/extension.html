Cascading telescoping extension

Mechanism design · Linear motion

FTC subsystemFTC Team 8393

My roleMechanism design

A multi-stage extension that reaches full height in about one second and carries 10 lb at the end.

Demo GIF or video

Problem

The robot needed rapid vertical reach that stayed rigid under load and was easy to service.

My contribution

Designed a threaded-rod first stage with a string-driven cascade on every stage after it. Used aluminum extrusion with nylon and carbon-fiber prints at high-stress points, modeled to ±0.25 mm clearances.

Result

Driven by a 3.7:1 goBILDA motor, it reaches full extension in about one second, carries up to 10 lb, and each stage swaps out with 4 screws.

Details

  • Low-friction bearings couple each stage for synchronized motion and even force distribution.
  • Self-lubricating bearings reduce wear.
  • Four screws per stage allow internal mechanisms to be replaced.
  • Cascade rigging
  • Tolerancing
  • Modular design
