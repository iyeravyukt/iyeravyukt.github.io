LogistiBot autonomous putaway robot

Mobile robotics · Computer vision

Sep 2023–Sep 2024Independent research

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

A mobile robot that finds, collects, and puts away materials on its own with over 75% accuracy.

LogistiBot robot
AprilTag navigation run
Demo GIF or video

Problem

Putting materials away inside warehouses is a bottleneck, and human error accounts for over 80% of inbound logistics failures (Wrobel, 2021). Most existing automation is fixed in place and expensive.

My contribution

Designed, built, and programmed a robot that drives to AprilTag-marked locations, collects the designated materials with a 4-bar linkage pivot arm, and unloads them in a target zone, using Java and OpenCV.

Result

Over 75% task accuracy. 12 awards, including U.S. Office of Naval Research STEM Honoree and National STEM Festival Finalist.

Details

  • Drives autonomously until it detects a designated object or AprilTag.
  • Collects the materials marked by that tag.
  • Unloads the collected materials in a specific zone.
