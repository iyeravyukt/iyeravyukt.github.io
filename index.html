ARISE: Adaptive Robotic Intelligent Support Exoskeleton

Independent Researcher and Inventor · Aug 2024 to present · U.S. provisional patent pending

Workers in healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics face high rates of musculoskeletal disorders from repetitive lifting. About 2.6 million workplace injuries occur in these sectors each year, roughly 30% involve the upper body, and they cost employers about $171 billion a year. Existing supports are rigid and don't adapt to the load.

  • Designed and fabricated a low-cost upper-body exoskeleton with two 13 N·m powered joints, a bearing-supported passive shoulder, and 25 laser-cut components.
  • Integrated EMG, load-cell, pulse-oximeter, and encoder feedback with PID control so assistance scales with the load in real time.

Custom wheels for traction on Martian terrain

Independent Researcher and Inventor · 3D-printed PLA+, tested in MMS-1 Mars simulant

Mars rovers lose mobility to fine regolith and uneven ground. Wheels on Curiosity and Perseverance have suffered punctures, slippage, and sand buildup.

  • Designed, 3D-printed, and traction-tested three wheel prototypes in MMS-1 Mars simulant, iterating after each round.
  • The third prototype performed best, with sand-filtering boxes for traction, V-shaped tread beams for side-to-side grip, and beam placement tuned to balance traction against weight.

MIT Beaver Works: underwater robotics

ROV perception: object detection and 3D pose estimation

BlueROV2 · Python, ROS, YOLOv8 · Raspberry Pi · Final challenge: autonomous light tag between robots

  • Trained a YOLOv8 detector on 3,000+ labeled frames from the ROV camera across varied lighting, turbidity, and occlusion, with histogram equalization and Gaussian noise augmentation.
  • Reached 95% classification accuracy with real-time inference on a Raspberry Pi.
  • Developed a geometry-based pose estimator that computes the target's depth, distance, and bearing from bounding-box position and known camera parameters, with no second model or extra sensors.
  • Integrated both into the ROV's ROS control system for real-time trajectory correction, then tuned them on live pool data.

AUV torpedo launcher

86% launch success in submerged trials · Tested to 15 ft depth

  • Designed and built a pin-actuated launcher with a pressure-regulated air reservoir, magnetic pin, and reinforced barrel, kept waterproof to protect the vehicle's electronics.
  • A magnetic switch drives a needle, nested in a 3D-printed housing, that punctures the air canister to fire.
  • Refined torpedo geometry through iterative testing to reduce backflow turbulence and keep propulsion consistent across depths.
Custom FTC subsystems

Mechanism design

Compact 1:1 bevel differential gearbox

FTC 2024–25, V3 scoring system · Photos

Symmetric bevel-gear differential that splits torque between two Axon MAX servos to control pitch and roll in a very tight footprint. Built from FR4 walls, a 3D-printed housing, custom printed bevel gears, 32 mm flanged bearings, and an 8 mm REX hub. Housing mass was cut through CAD iteration while holding torque under load.

Cascading telescoping extension

FTC vertical extension

Multi-stage extension with a threaded-rod first stage and a string-driven cascade on every stage after it. Aluminum extrusion stages with nylon and carbon-fiber prints at high-stress points, modeled to ±0.25 mm clearances. Each stage swaps out with 4 screws. Driven by a 3.7:1 goBILDA motor, it reaches full extension in about one second and carries up to 10 lb at the end.

Internally rotating transfer

FTC 2023–24, Centerstage

Moves game pieces from intake to scorer through an internal rotating interface instead of a bulky external transfer. A passive compliant roller mounted perpendicular to the powered rollers orients each 3-inch pixel within about three inches of space. Reached 100% transfer accuracy in testing.
