ARISE: Adaptive Robotic Intelligent Support Exoskeleton

Mechanical design · Biosensing · Control

Independent research · Aug 2024 to present · U.S. provisional patent pending

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

A low-cost upper-body exoskeleton that scales its assistance to the load in real time during repetitive lifting.

Problem

Workers in healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics face high rates of musculoskeletal disorders from repetitive lifting. Roughly 30% of the 2.6 million yearly workplace injuries in these sectors involve the upper body. Existing supports are rigid and don't adapt to the load.

My contribution

Designed and fabricated the exoskeleton with two 13 N·m powered joints, a bearing-supported passive shoulder, and 25 laser-cut components. Integrated EMG, load-cell, pulse-oximeter, and encoder feedback with PID control.

Result

−13%EMG voltage
−53.9%Pulse rate
−77%Perceived exertion
300+Trials

Regeneron ISEF Biomedical Engineering Finalist. PJAS 1st Place, State and Regional. 8 awards in total.

  • Exoskeleton design
  • EMG and load sensing
  • PID control

LogistiBot: autonomous material handling and putaway robot

Mobile robotics · Computer vision · Mechanism design

Independent research · Sep 2023 to Sep 2024

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

A mobile robot that finds, collects, and puts away materials on its own using AprilTag navigation.

Problem

Putting materials away inside warehouses is a bottleneck, and human error accounts for over 80% of inbound logistics failures (Wrobel, 2021). Most existing automation is fixed in place and expensive.

My contribution

Designed, built, and programmed a robot that drives to AprilTag-marked locations, collects the designated materials with a 4-bar linkage pivot arm, and unloads them in a target zone, using Java and OpenCV.

Result

Reached over 75% task accuracy. 12 awards, including U.S. Office of Naval Research STEM Honoree, National STEM Festival Finalist, and 1st Place at the Pittsburgh Regional Science & Engineering Fair.

  • Java
  • OpenCV and AprilTags
  • 4-bar linkage

ROV perception: object detection and 3D pose estimation

Perception · Machine learning · ROS

MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute · Jun to Aug 2025

My roleAutonomous Underwater Vehicle Student Researcher

A perception pipeline that lets a BlueROV2 find another robot and estimate its relative pose in real time.

Problem

The final challenge was autonomous light tag between underwater robots, which needs reliable detection and relative pose in changing lighting, turbidity, and occlusion.

My contribution

Trained a YOLOv8 detector on 3,000+ labeled frames from the ROV camera, with histogram equalization and Gaussian noise augmentation. Developed a geometry-based estimator that computes depth, distance, and bearing from the bounding box and known camera parameters, with no second model or extra sensors. Integrated both into the ROS control system.

Result

95% classification accuracy with real-time inference on a Raspberry Pi, and real-time relative pose during pool tests.

  • YOLOv8
  • Python and ROS
  • Pose estimation

Custom wheels for traction on Martian terrain

Mechanical design · Prototyping · Testing

Independent research

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

Three 3D-printed rover wheel designs, traction-tested in Mars simulant.

Problem

Mars rovers lose mobility to fine regolith and uneven ground. Wheels on Curiosity and Perseverance have suffered punctures, slippage, and sand buildup.

My contribution

Designed, 3D-printed in PLA+, and tested three wheel prototypes in MMS-1 Mars simulant, iterating after each round.

Result

The third prototype performed best, with sand-filtering boxes for traction, V-shaped tread beams for side-to-side grip, and beam placement tuned to balance traction against weight.

  • CAD
  • 3D printing
  • Traction testing

AUV torpedo launcher

Mechanical design · Pneumatics · Underwater systems

MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute · Jun to Aug 2025

My roleDesigner and builder

A pin-actuated compressed-air launcher for repeatable projectile deployment from an autonomous underwater vehicle.

Problem

The launcher had to fire consistently at varying depths while staying waterproof and protecting the vehicle's electronics.

My contribution

Designed and built the launcher with a pressure-regulated air reservoir, magnetic pin, and reinforced barrel. A magnetic switch drives a needle, nested in a 3D-printed housing, that punctures the air canister. Refined the torpedo geometry through iterative testing to reduce backflow turbulence.

Result

86%Launch success, submerged
15 ftMax tested depth

Compact 1:1 bevel differential gearbox

Mechanism design · Power transmission

FTC Team 8393 · 2024–25 V3 scoring system

My roleDesigner

A differential that controls pitch and roll from two servos inside a very tight footprint.

Problem

The scoring system needed two synchronized rotational axes in a space too small for a conventional spur-gear transmission.

My contribution

Designed a symmetric bevel-gear differential that splits torque between two Axon MAX servos. Built from FR4 walls, a 3D-printed housing, custom printed bevel gears, 32 mm flanged bearings, and an 8 mm REX hub.

Result

Cut housing mass through CAD iteration while holding torque under load. Ran on the 2024–25 V3 competition robot.

  • Bevel gears
  • CAD
  • 3D printing

Cascading telescoping extension

Mechanism design · Linear motion

FTC vertical extension

My roleDesigner

A fast, lightweight multi-stage extension with a high reach-to-base ratio.

Problem

The robot needed rapid vertical reach that stayed rigid under load and was easy to service.

My contribution

Designed a threaded-rod first stage with a string-driven cascade on every stage after it. Used aluminum extrusion with nylon and carbon-fiber prints at high-stress points, modeled to ±0.25 mm clearances. Each stage swaps out with 4 screws.

Result

Driven by a 3.7:1 goBILDA motor, it reaches full extension in about one second and carries up to 10 lb at the end.

  • Cascade rigging
  • Tolerancing
  • Modular design

Internally rotating transfer

Mechanism design · Packaging

FTC Team 22312 · 2023–24 Centerstage

My roleDesigner

A compact handoff between intake and scorer that fits in about three inches of space.

Problem

External transfer systems are bulky and interfere with other subsystems, and each 3-inch game piece had to arrive in a set orientation.

My contribution

Moved the rotating interface inside the robot and mounted a passive compliant roller perpendicular to the powered rollers to orient each piece.

Result

Reached 100% transfer accuracy in testing after iterative prototyping.

  • Compliant rollers
  • Prototyping
  • Space-constrained design
