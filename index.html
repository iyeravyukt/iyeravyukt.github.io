ARISE adaptive exoskeleton

Mechanical design · Biosensing · Control

Aug 2024–present · U.S. patent pendingIndependent research

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

A low-cost upper-body exoskeleton cut perceived exertion by 77% across 300+ lifting trials.

Project details →

Problem

Workers in healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics face high rates of musculoskeletal disorders from repetitive lifting, and existing supports are rigid and do not adapt to the load.

My contribution

Designed and fabricated the exoskeleton with two 13 N·m powered joints, a bearing-supported passive shoulder, and 25 laser-cut components. Integrated EMG, load-cell, pulse-oximeter, and encoder feedback with PID control.

Result

EMG voltage down 13%, pulse rate down 53.9%, and perceived exertion down 77% across 300+ trials. Regeneron ISEF Biomedical Engineering Finalist and 8 awards in total.

  • Exoskeleton design
  • EMG and load sensing
  • PID control

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

A mobile robot that finds, collects, and puts away materials on its own with over 75% accuracy.

Project details →

Problem

Putting materials away inside warehouses is a bottleneck, and human error accounts for over 80% of inbound logistics failures (Wrobel, 2021). Most existing automation is fixed in place and expensive.

My contribution

Designed, built, and programmed a robot that drives to AprilTag-marked locations, collects the designated materials with a 4-bar linkage pivot arm, and unloads them in a target zone, using Java and OpenCV.

Result

Over 75% task accuracy. 12 awards, including U.S. Office of Naval Research STEM Honoree and National STEM Festival Finalist.

ROV detection and pose estimation

Perception · Machine learning

Jun–Aug 2025MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute

My rolePerception model training & pose estimation

A YOLOv8 detector and geometric pose estimator ran in real time on a Raspberry Pi with 95% accuracy.

Project details →

Problem

The final challenge was autonomous light tag between underwater robots, which needs reliable detection and relative pose in changing lighting, turbidity, and occlusion.

My contribution

Trained a YOLOv8 detector on 3,000+ labeled frames from the ROV camera. Developed a geometry-based estimator that computes depth, distance, and bearing from the bounding box and known camera parameters, and integrated both into the ROS control system.

Result

95% classification accuracy with real-time inference on a Raspberry Pi, and real-time relative pose during pool tests. Selected for the program at under 9% acceptance.

  • YOLOv8
  • Python and ROS
  • Pose estimation

Wheels for Martian terrain

Mechanical design · Testing

Independent researchPittsburgh, PA

My roleIndependent Researcher and Inventor

Three 3D-printed rover wheel designs, traction-tested in MMS-1 Mars simulant.

Project details →

Problem

Mars rovers lose mobility to fine regolith and uneven ground. Wheels on Curiosity and Perseverance have suffered punctures, slippage, and sand buildup.

My contribution

Designed, 3D-printed in PLA+, and tested three wheel prototypes in MMS-1 Mars simulant, iterating after each round.

Result

The third prototype achieved the best traction, with sand-filtering boxes and V-shaped multidirectional tread beams, while balancing weight.

  • CAD
  • 3D printing
  • Traction testing

A 1:1 differential that controls pitch and roll from two servos inside a very tight footprint.

Cascading telescoping extension

Mechanism design · Linear motion

FTC subsystemFTC Team 8393

My roleMechanism design

A multi-stage extension that reaches full height in about one second and carries 10 lb at the end.

Project details →

Problem

The robot needed rapid vertical reach that stayed rigid under load and was easy to service.

My contribution

Designed a threaded-rod first stage with a string-driven cascade on every stage after it. Used aluminum extrusion with nylon and carbon-fiber prints at high-stress points, modeled to ±0.25 mm clearances.

Result

Driven by a 3.7:1 goBILDA motor, it reaches full extension in about one second, carries up to 10 lb, and each stage swaps out with 4 screws.

  • Cascade rigging
  • Tolerancing
  • Modular design
